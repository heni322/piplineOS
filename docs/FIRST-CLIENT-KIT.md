# First client kit

The goal is one signed pilot. The fastest route is **people you can reach personally**, not cold email at scale:
- the system isn't sending live yet;
- a first client says yes to a person they trust.

## 1. Who (the first 10)
UK IT managed service providers, 10–200 staff, founder-led (ICP #1, `docs/GO-TO-MARKET.md` §3). Where to find
them:
- your own network and LinkedIn connections;
- local business groups and IT channel events;
- partner programmes (Microsoft partners in your region);
- or ask: "Who do you know who runs an MSP?"

Keep a simple list:

| # | Company | Person | How I know them | Contacted | Call booked | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 2. First message (personal, one to one, written by you)
> Hi [first name], I'm building a service that books first meetings for IT providers with UK companies that have
> just won a public contract, are hiring IT staff or have raised funding. You approve every email before it goes,
> and you pay only for meetings that actually happen with someone who meets criteria you set. I'm looking for
> **3 pilot partners** at a reduced price, in exchange for honest feedback. Would 20 minutes next week be useful?

Short. One question. No attachment in the first message.

## 3. The 20-minute call
1. **Them first (10 min).**
   - How do you win new clients today?
   - What's a new client worth to you per year?
   - What does a good first meeting look like?
   - Who is your ideal buyer?
2. **Show it (5 min).**
   - The landing page.
   - The demo portal: results, meetings with their evidence, the emergency stop.
   - The point to make: you see every email before it goes.
3. **The offer (5 min).** See §4. Ask: "Shall we set it up?"

## 4. The pilot offer (from GO-TO-MARKET §2)
- **£490 once.** Covers the domain, 3 mailboxes, DNS, the 21-day warm-up, set-up and **300 contacts**.
- **£250 per qualified, attended meeting**, judged against criteria **they** sign in the portal.
- **Guarantee:** fewer than 3 positive replies from 300 people means the fee is credited or refunded.
  `LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED` on the wording.
- **Timing:** about 3 weeks of warm-up, then 14 days of sending. Check-in at day 5; review at day 14.
- **Their control:** they approve every email, can stop everything from their portal in one click, and get a
  weekly results email.

## 5. Objections and honest answers
- **"We tried lead-gen agencies."** "You approve every email, and you pay for meetings that took place with
  someone who meets criteria you signed. If it doesn't work, the guarantee covers you."
- **"Is this legal / will it hurt our reputation?"** "We write only to companies, never to sole traders. Every email
  has one-click unsubscribe. A solicitor is reviewing our position before we send. Your main domain is never used;
  we send from a separate domain."
- **"What results do you get?"** Be honest: "You'd be one of the first. Here's how we measure it: replies and
  meetings per 100 people reached. That's why the pilot is priced low and has a guarantee."
- **"Why the set-up fee?"** "It pays for the domain, mailboxes and warm-up, which are real costs before the first
  email."

**Never claim results you don't have.** The landing page doesn't either.

## 6. After the yes
1. Send them the terms and DPA. `LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED`: use the solicitor-approved versions.
2. Onboard them in the console (Clients → Add client). The **Next step** guide walks through everything: LIA, DPA,
   offer and pricing, client login, sending domain, activation.
3. They accept the criteria and pricing in their portal, and can add their own target accounts.
4. Create the campaign. It is in Pilot Mode automatically; set the limit to 300.
5. Day 0 to 21: warm-up. Day 21 onwards: send, approve every email, and watch the numbers per 100 reached.
